Privacy Policy

App: Pray to Unlock. Last updated: November 26, 2026.

What the app does

The app shows a short prayer before apps you choose can open. Everything happens on your phone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Nothing is sent off your device.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings (prayer style, the apps you chose, unlock time) and your prayer counts and streak are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Permissions

  • Usage access: used only to notice when you open an app you chose. This information is never stored long-term or shared.
  • Display over other apps: used only to show the prayer screen.
  • Notifications: used for the ongoing notice that keeps the lock running.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Contact

Questions: use the contact email on the app's Google Play page.

Política de privacidad

La app Pray to Unlock no recopila datos. No tiene cuenta, anuncios, analíticas ni servidores. Tus ajustes y tu racha se guardan solo en tu teléfono y se borran al desinstalar la app. El acceso al uso sirve solo para notar cuándo abres una app elegida; mostrar sobre otras apps sirve solo para mostrar la pantalla de oración; las notificaciones mantienen el bloqueo activo. Contacto: usa el correo de contacto de la página de la app en Google Play.
